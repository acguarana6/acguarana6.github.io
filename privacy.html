Privacy

What this site records about visits, and how to change your choice.

Who is responsible

This is the personal website of Alexandra Chin. I decide what is collected here and why, and you can reach me through the contact page.

Visit counts

This site uses GoatCounter to count visits. It records the page and sections viewed, the site you came from, your country, and your browser, device type and screen size. It sets no cookies and doesn't build a profile of you. I use these counts to see which parts of the site are useful, which is my legitimate interest in running it.

Optional analytics

If you choose "Allow analytics", the site also loads Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. They set cookies, recognise a returning browser, and record approximate location, time on the page, clicks and scrolling. Clarity can replay a visit as a recording; what you type into the contact form is masked. Neither is used for advertising here. If you choose "No thanks", neither one is loaded.

These run only with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time with the button below. Withdrawing switches both services off and deletes the cookies they set on this site.

Contact form

When you send a message, your name, email address and message are delivered to my inbox through Web3Forms. I use them only to reply to you, and keep them for as long as our conversation needs.

Who receives the data and how long it is kept

  • GoatCounter receives the visit counts. They contain nothing that identifies you.
  • Google receives Google Analytics data. Visitor-level data is kept for 2 months. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft receives Clarity data and keeps it for the periods in the Microsoft privacy statement.
  • Web3Forms passes contact messages on to my email inbox.

These companies may process data outside your country, including in the United States. I don't sell this data or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of what is held about you, ask me to correct or delete it, and withdraw your analytics consent at any time. If you are in the EU or UK and think your data has been mishandled, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Questions

For any of the above, send me a message.
