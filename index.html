About

I'm a software engineer with more than three years of experience building field-deployed systems for government customers. I work directly with end users, turning field requirements into tailored software, and I'm often the glue between cross-functional teams.

Before that I did human-computer interaction research at the MIT Media Lab and EPFL, and studied computer science and mathematics at Wellesley College. I'm now looking to bring hands-on technical experience and customer-facing problem-solving to an innovative role.

Experience

  1. Aug 2023 to present

    Software Engineer

    MIT Lincoln Laboratory, Homeland Protection Systems (Groups 46 and 45), Lexington, MA

    • Hardened end-to-end deployment processes from development machines to edge devices (Raspberry Pi 2, Pi 5, Jetson, i.MX8) using Ansible and Docker.
    • Designed and delivered a hardware/software prototype (Raspberry Pi, network switches, ZMQ messaging bus, React), from interviewing device operators about their workflow, through building the web application with them in a tight feedback loop, to running the full stack on physical hardware.
    • Validated signal integrity across a cybersecurity shim from radio controller to flight controller (STM32 dev boards, RadioMaster and XBee radios, custom breakout and logic boards), using a logic analyzer to confirm correct packet and telemetry flow and debugging across the software-firmware boundary.
    • Coordinated with sensor and algorithm teams to rapidly integrate new capabilities into a modular US air defense software system.
    • Configured the tactical display front end and multi-source correlator tracker for an operational Java application that routed track data from aircraft and unmanned aerial systems.
    • Conducted on-site engineering checkouts and supported flight tests to tune radar, convert and transfer data, and configure operator displays, working with operators, field service, IT and software teams to match data presentation to real operational needs.
  2. Jun 2021 to Aug 2021

    Research Intern, Summer@EPFL

    CHILI Lab (Computer-Human Interaction in Learning and Instruction), EPFL, Lausanne, Switzerland

    • Helped design and run a study on collaborative robot-student learning, coordinating directly with student participants.
    • Analyzed robot-student collaboration data (pandas, matplotlib, statistical methods) and presented findings to the research team to inform the next stage of the study.
    • Co-authored a paper accepted to IEEE RO-MAN 2022 and nominated for Best Student Paper.
  3. Jun 2020 to Jun 2021

    UROP Researcher

    MIT Media Lab, Cambridge, MA

    • Led user studies and UX design for Project Us, a communications technology suite that uses physiological and audio signals to promote empathy between users.
    • Met directly with study participants to gather feedback and iterate on NLP algorithms and hardware/software prototypes.
    • Co-authored a paper published at CHI 2022.
