About

I'm a software engineer with 3+ years of experience building field-deployed systems for government customers, and I do my best work where software meets hardware and real users. Throughout my career, I have supported on-site field deployments and flight tests alongside operators, software teams, hardware teams, and IT. I've also designed and delivered hardware/software prototypes end-to-end, and hardened deployment pipelines to edge devices. I'm adaptable, a fast learner, and a genuine collaborator who enjoys being the glue between cross-functional teams.

Experience

    Software Engineer

    MIT Lincoln Laboratory, Homeland Protection Systems (Groups 46 and 45), Lexington, MA
